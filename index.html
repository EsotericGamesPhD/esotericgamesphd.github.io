Synthient

A free, open-source music workstation with Claude built in.

Describe what you want (add a breakdown, make the bass fatter, brass on the hook) and Claude writes, arranges and mixes the parts while you listen. It's the strange idea that isn't a game.

About the studio

Esoteric Games is a solo indie studio. It started with typing games (Typers Combat, Typomon Hunter) and now makes whatever strange idea comes next: a beekeeping simulator, mutant basketball, a two-pilot mech satire and a body-grid auto-battler.
